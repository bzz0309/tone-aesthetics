# Prompt Set Summary｜Four Asian Casting Routes

## Generation method

使用内置图像生成／编辑能力。01 为身份保持式编辑；02–04 为依据已提供参考图抽象出的原创人物。参考图只提供五官结构、妆发和摄影方向，不复制真人身份。

## Shared prompt lock

- Original fictional adult Asian woman, age 18–30.
- Vertical 4:5 head-and-shoulders casting portrait.
- Plain fitted black crew-neck T-shirt, neutral gray studio background.
- Direct gaze, closed relaxed mouth, restrained makeup, realistic pores.
- No influencer face, pointed V chin, doll eyes, idol stage makeup, plastic skin, jewelry, logos, text or watermark.

## Route deltas

### 01 Rounded Feline Lob

Soft compact rounded oval; fuller cheeks; short blunt chin; narrow lifted cat eyes; low straight brows; small rounded nose tip; compact full lips; center-part collarbone lob.

### 02 Sleek Signal

Long narrow oval; high lateral cheekbones; straight lower jaw; narrow heavy-lidded almond eyes; longer natural nose; high sleek ponytail.

### 03 Soft Pulse Y2K

Broad soft oval; full cheeks; open natural almond eyes; short rounded nose; wide natural lips; very long dark-brown waves with irregular airy fringe.

### 04 Nocturne Volume

Unmistakably East Asian; broad sculpted oval; pronounced cheekbones; wider jaw and blunt chin; deep-set hooded eyes; medium-low bridge; very long side-part espresso waves; restrained smoked taupe makeup.
