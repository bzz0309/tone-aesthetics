# Prompt Summary

## Mode

内置图像编辑，identity-preserve。

## Target

Image 1 为唯一编辑底图和身份基准；人物保持原创，不复制或冒充任何真实公众人物。

## Controlled changes

- compact soft oval-to-heart face;
- fuller upper cheeks and subtle cheek apples;
- moderately sized elongated cat-like almond eyes with softly lifted outer corners;
- slightly lower, straighter natural brows;
- softly rounded natural nose tip;
- subtly fuller central lips and relaxed closed mouth;
- quiet charisma with playful undertone.

## Invariants

保持年龄、眼距、脸宽、五官位置、发际线、发型、服装、姿势、构图、背景与光线。

## Avoid

真实公众人物复刻、明星替身、放大眼睛、厚双眼皮、尖下巴、重修容、玻璃肌、舞台妆、磨皮、换脸、换服装或换背景。
