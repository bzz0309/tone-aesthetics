# Prompt Set Summary｜Five Asian Routes

## Method

使用内置图像生成／编辑能力。01来自身份保持式微调；02–05为依据用户参考图提炼的原创亚洲角色。参考图仅用于五官结构、妆发和影像气质，不复制真人身份。

## Shared casting lock

- Original fictional adult Asian woman, apparent age 18–30.
- Premium independent fashion casting portrait, vertical 4:5.
- Plain black crew-neck top, neutral gray studio backdrop.
- Real skin texture, direct calm gaze, restrained makeup.
- Avoid influencer face, idol stage makeup, pointed V chin, doll eyes, plastic skin, logos, text and watermark.

## Route additions

- **01 Cool Feline**：compact rounded oval, full cheeks, short blunt chin, narrow lifted cat eyes, collarbone lob.
- **02 Androgynous Signal**：long narrow oval, high cheekbones, straighter jaw, narrow heavy-lidded eyes, sleek high ponytail.
- **03 Natural Current**：broad soft oval, full cheeks, natural open almond eyes, short rounded nose, loose long waves with airy fringe.
- **04 Asian Nocturne**：broad sculpted East Asian oval, pronounced cheekbones, wider jaw, deep-set hooded eyes, dramatic side-part long waves.
- **05 Sweet Voltage**：compact diamond-heart face, wider upper cheekbones, slightly wider-set lifted eyes, broken-arch brows, high half-up long hair with micro-braids.
